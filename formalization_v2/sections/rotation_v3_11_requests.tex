\subsection{Requests, prepared input, and retained data}
\label{sec:requests}
A request is \defterm{admissible} when it passes the application's admission
checks. Its logical identity distinguishes retransmission from a new
client command. Let $\operatorname{enc}(x)$ be its canonical authenticated
representation: transport wrappers and alternate encodings of the same
authentication proof do not create a new request. Each node retains
requests in first-receipt FIFO order; duplicates preserve that order.
Different nodes need not have the same order.

A request leaves the pending store only when its identity occurs in $G$.
Regional commitment, regular handoff and a discarded speculative suffix
do not remove it. The prepared primary below occupies the first entry of
the first own-session DATA when its certificate is nonempty. After that
primary, and at descendant positions, a correct leader fills by ordinary
local FIFO, skipping identities already in the exact parent ancestry or
batch and rechecking application validity after earlier batch entries.
This changes the selection rule, not input delivery or common ordering.

\paragraph{Individual request relay.}
On first complete receipt of an authenticated admissible request $x$, a
correct node persists its pending entry and its ownership journal record.
For every authenticated installed snapshot of its physical region in
which it is a member, it dispatches the full request once per request and
snapshot. Installing a snapshot schedules the same individual relays for
retained requests. The persistent owner and hydration rules in
\cref{sec:request-ownership} supply the specified historical discovery
and changing-roster delivery path. A relay alone does not assert
admissibility after every future parent.

These stores and duties survive Waiting, Ready, Active, Frozen, Completed,
Terminated, Closed and \texttt{RequestClose}. They create no BFT vote,
lock, timer or entry. A dispatched admitted complete regional message
uses the original intra-region delivery-and-processing bound, including
old and new correct AUTH members in the same physical region. The bound
does not make an arbitrary backlog, fragmented pool, native job or
roster-discovery task finish in one $\Delta$.

\subsubsection{The first-DATA report}
\label{sec:input-report}
For a completely validated effective parent $P$, let
$\operatorname{OwnDATA}_{\beta,r}(P)$ be the DATA blocks of session
$(\beta,r)$ in the ancestry from its fixed opening $A$ through $P$.
The original parent and ancestry rules determine this list; a local
current-$G$ test or commit flag does not. If it is empty, the next DATA
is the \defterm{first own-session DATA} and executes immediately after
$A$. It must execute a nonempty opening certificate's selected body
first. An empty certificate permits ordinary valid batching, including
an empty batch. If the list is nonempty, its complete ancestry carries
and validates the first own-session DATA and its input witness.

A speculative first DATA discharges this obligation only on its own
descendants. Recovery returning to $A$ reenforces it with a matching
opening certificate; recovery retaining a descendant retains the earlier
first-DATA witness. ANCHOR-only histories cannot discharge it. DRAIN
requires the original positive DATA quota to have been reached and
therefore cannot bypass it. All relevant histories, receipts and
Completion paths apply the same ancestry rule.

\paragraph{Context and the initializer's choice.}
For $V=(\beta,r,g,t)$ and $P=A_{\beta,r,g,t}$, the full report context
$\mathit{RC}$ binds the chain, protocol, type \tagg{INPUT\_REPORT}, session,
assigned region, configuration, $\tagg{AUTH}_{r,g}$ reference,
$(g,t,j=0)$, the generation's opening identity, and $P$'s hash, DATA height
and physical position. Original admission authenticates the boundary and
authority; a claimed pair alone is insufficient. Alternate QC encodings
do not change this context.

When $\operatorname{OwnDATA}_{\beta,r}(P)$ is empty, the initializer
chooses one complete matching valid $\mathit{OC}$ from its prepared cache.
It persistently signs the separate regional sidecar
\[
 \langle\tagg{INPUT\_ADVERTISE},\mathit{RC},
    \Hash(\operatorname{canon}(\mathit{OC})),k(\mathit{OC})\rangle_{L(V)},
\]
where $k(\mathit{OC})$ is its selected key or \tagg{EMPTY}.
A correct initializer signs one advertisement per $\mathit{RC}$ and
resends it unchanged. It supplies that advertisement and the complete
certificate beside the original NEWVIEW. Initial term zero uses the
Ready attachment; a recovery returning to $A$ uses the same cache.
A descendant recovery supplies its historical first-DATA witness.
An ordinary term after terminal DATA already has own-session DATA.

The advertisement binds the initializer's exact choice during forwarding.
A relay cannot replace it with another valid certificate. Different
advertisements from a Byzantine initializer may split reports and stall
its term; they create no new Quit proof. The original signed NEWVIEW
bytes and original equivocation predicate remain unchanged.

\paragraph{Emission.}
A correct member reports at ordinary-term entry for $t>0$, after validating
the preceding terminal QC and exact parent, and at complete NEWVIEW
validation for $t=0$ in its entered generation. Require the original
fresh-action gate, current entered unfrozen generation and DATA-producing
term. At that event:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),itemsep=2pt]
\item If $\operatorname{OwnDATA}_{\beta,r}(P)$ is empty, check the
initializer's advertisement, its exact $\mathit{RC}$ and signature,
the complete matching $\mathit{OC}$ under \tagg{CheckOC}, and the signed
certificate digest/key. Emit
$\tagg{INPUT\_REPORT}(\mathit{RC},\tagg{OPENING},
\Hash(\operatorname{canon}(\mathit{OC})),k(\mathit{OC}))$.
\item Otherwise validate the inherited first-DATA witness and emit
\[
 \tagg{INPUT\_REPORT}(\mathit{RC},\tagg{DISCHARGED},\tagg{EMPTY}).
\]
\end{enumerate}
Sign and persist at most one report for $\mathit{RC}$ before sending it
to $L(V)$; resends repeat it exactly. Reports contain no private pool,
request body or second copy of the certificate. Later arrivals do not
amend the report. A missing or invalid opening advertisement blocks only
REPORT; a later complete valid advertisement may finish the action while
its original fresh-action gate still permits it. No timer is reset.
Historical validation never creates a missing fresh signature.

Immediate original NEWVIEW forwarding, ANCHOR votes and original evidence
inspection remain independent. Parse shared packets independently:
invalid input sidecars cannot suppress a valid ANCHOR vote or original
Quit evidence. Reports, offers and advertisements create no QC rank,
lock, commitment, entry permission, progress timer or new Quit proof.
Fresh report and advertisement signatures obey the retirement gate;
existing witness retention and relay do not reopen that gate.

\paragraph{Collection and common validation.}
At an opening parent, a correct leader collects distinct-member reports
of weight $>F$ for its advertised certificate's exact digest, key and
$\mathit{RC}$. It checks membership and signatures against that one
already validated certificate. At a descendant parent it collects
matching \tagg{DISCHARGED}, \tagg{EMPTY} reports. Correct reports alone
have weight $W-B\ge W-F>F$. Once the reports and original parent/ANCHOR
QC are available, propose the first DATA: execute a nonempty advertised
certificate's body as primary, then fill by ordinary FIFO; use ordinary
valid batching if the certificate is empty or the obligation is
discharged. The singleton fit check ensures the primary fits.

Every DATA proposal at $j=0$ requires original full validation and a
distinct-member report set of weight $>F$ for its exact historical
$\mathit{RC}$. If its own-session parent list is empty, require one
complete matching $\mathit{OC}$, the initializer's matching advertisement,
and reports all signing that same \tagg{OPENING} digest/key. A nonempty
selected body is the first batch entry, with its canonical bytes,
admission at $A$, individual fit and ancestry exclusion checked; exclude
another occurrence of its identity in the batch. If the parent list is
nonempty, require matching \tagg{DISCHARGED} reports and the validated
inherited first-DATA witness. Later positions and DRAIN retain that
required ancestry. Apply these rules in Vote, NEWVIEW/recovery histories,
own-session receipt processing and Completion.

Validators never require their own offer or report, compare a witness
with their private queue or attached certificate, or apply current-time
or current-$G$ tests to a historical witness. The selected body and
signature sets are supplied common inputs. Earlier-term proposals in
the current generation retain the original Vote eligibility after local
advancement; old/future generations keep their archival/buffering rules.
Evidence-first processing always inspects original signed material even
when a sidecar is incomplete or invalid. Different valid certificate,
advertisement/report signer sets or encodings are witness variants:
proposal identity, deduplication and timer identity stay
$(\beta,r,g,t,j,\Hash(B))$. A different selected request changes $B$ and
is governed by the original proposal/equivocation rules.

\subsubsection{Regional evidence and the original public result}
\label{sec:input-report-carriage}
Write $R_0$ for the original complete regional result of
\cref{sec:delivery}, with every original DATA, FINAL, proposal, QC,
ancestry, boundary, authority, native-validation and public-source object
required there. It is not merely the public kernel. The augmented regional
bundle is $R_+=(R_0,W_R)$, where $W_R$ supplies the opening certificate,
initializer advertisement and report sidecars required by augmented
own-session validation. Correct senders retain and forward complete
$R_+$. STATUS/NEWVIEW histories, recovery, receipt relays, Completion and
retirement transfers preserve the applicable witnesses. References must
resolve within the same complete regional message. A public receipt
alone need not provide augmented own-session Completion inputs.

The Publish Layer input remains exactly $R_0$. Erasure removes only the
new separately framed regional sidecars. Original signed NEWVIEW,
PROPOSE/DATA, vote, QC and FINAL bytes, hashes and public denotations
are unchanged. For the same DATA and original witness the public record
has the same old format, with zero added offer, advertisement or REPORT
bytes. The selected request uses ordinary DATA capacity. Original public
size/load limits and the original $T_{\rm pub}$ start event are unchanged;
no bare request or preparation certificate gains a publication bound.

The honest-FINAL argument traces an actual correct signature to a
timer-origin Vote that checked original validity and this augmented
predicate. It establishes existence of a genuine historical first-DATA
witness, without making it a public fetch dependency. FINAL still binds
no recovery or term and authenticates no publisher-chosen alternate
witness. Cross-session inherited anchors retain the historical kernel
rule. Global extension, branch, reference and Force-cutoff checks remain
independently necessary.

Keep block data and receipts needed for recovery and checkpoint checks.
Before finalized Espresso inclusion, keep complete $R_0$ available to
the Publish Layer and direct readers; afterward other regions may obtain
it from authenticated Espresso history. Regional sidecars remain with
their required data/evidence owners. Public erasure and global promotion
authorize no release of their last required copy.

\subsubsection{Report timing and request inclusion}
\label{sec:input-report-progress}
\begin{proposition}[Local report timing]
\label{prop:v38-report-timing}
\label{prop:v310-report-timing}
Under the original admitted complete-message/native-validation scope,
prepared-entry interface and active unfrozen/unclosed interval of the
correct-leader progress argument, the rule meets the original fresh
position deadlines. All correct members vote ordinary first DATA by
$4\Delta$ after first correct term entry; initial/recovery term zero
retains $6\Delta$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $e$ be first correct term entry. In an ordinary term, complete
forwarding of the preceding terminal proposal and correct votes
disseminates its QC and ancestry everywhere by $e+2\Delta$.
Own-session DATA is already in that ancestry, so each entry emits its
\tagg{DISCHARGED} report without a pool scan. Correct reports reach a
correct leader by $e+3\Delta$, and its complete first proposal is
processed and voted everywhere by $e+4\Delta$.

For term zero, the original prepared-entry skew is $\Delta$.
A correct initializer sends NEWVIEW by $e+3\Delta$, with its cached
certificate and signed advertisement if it returns to $A$, or the
inherited first-DATA witness otherwise. Complete processing by
$e+4\Delta$ sends independent ANCHOR votes and matching reports.
Both correct-only inputs reach the leader by $e+5\Delta$; first DATA
is voted everywhere by $e+6\Delta$. No private selection job or new
native restoration remains on this path. DRAIN creates no report.
Later positions retain $2\Delta$, so the $p$th fresh vote occurs by
$e+(2p+4)\Delta\le e_v+(2p+4)\Delta$. At equality, messages precede
progress callbacks.
\end{proof}

Complete forwarding supplies the same common predicate everywhere.
Protecting QCs, locks and timers therefore refer to actual
augmented-valid Votes. Report collection does not pause BLAME or the
faulty-term checks; terminal forwarding retains every dependency needed
for other correct votes. A correct initializer proposes by $e+5\Delta$
and retains the original protected window and complete-receipt bound
$e+9\Delta$. These statements require the concrete packet and handler
scope of \cref{sec:input-resource-scope}; finiteness alone does not prove
that footprint fits the old service profile.

\begin{proposition}[Request inclusion through session-first DATA]
\label{prop:session-input-progress}
\label{prop:v38-request-inclusion}
Let a correct persistent request owner in region $i$ actually receive
$x$ at finite time. Suppose $x$ fits alone and remains admissible at the
opening states of the accepted serving sessions considered until its
inclusion. If region $i$ contributes infinitely many accepted
positive-output sessions, then $x$ enters $G$ under the ownership and
preparation rules above.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Suppose $x$ never enters $G$. By \cref{prop:ownership-coverage}, all but
finitely many relevant authority slots are nonexceptional for $x$.
For each nonexceptional accepted positive-output session, contiguous
accepted ancestry includes its first own-session DATA $B$. Its exact
opening $A$ is an accepted ancestor, possibly accepted in the same
EXPORT. Since $x$ remains absent from $G$, it is absent from that
ancestry. By hypothesis it is eligible at $A$.

Accepted original commitment evidence contains a correct FINAL signer.
Trace that actual signature to its original timer-origin Vote, whose
complete regional inputs checked the first-own-session predicate.
Its genuine witness contains a valid opening certificate; a public
publisher's alternate annotations do not supply this provenance.
By \cref{prop:opening-coverage}, this certificate selects a real
request $y$ with $\operatorname{key}(y)\le\operatorname{key}(x)$.
The mandatory predicate makes $B$ execute $y$ first, with its identity
absent from $A$.

Let $\Phi_x(G)$ count distinct logical identities represented by
canonical byte strings of key at most $\operatorname{key}(x)$ that
are absent from $G$. It is finite and never increases as $G$ extends.
At the canonical acceptance of each counted $B$, its actual predecessor
is $A$, including any ancestors accepted in the same EXPORT, so $y$
spends a previously unaccepted identity and strictly decreases
$\Phi_x$. Later accepted ancestry cannot spend that identity again.
Speculative/discarded executions do not decrease this global potential;
Force and recovery never roll back $G$.

Each session has finite DATA quota. Zero-output retries add none;
positive-output closure or regular completion advances the slot.
Thus finitely many exceptional slots contribute only finitely many
accepted session-first DATA. Infinitely many remaining accepted
sessions would force infinitely many strict decreases of the finite
$\Phi_x$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

The counting unit is an accepted session-first DATA, not every
term-first report. Post-cut arrivals remain pending and use continuing
relay and later paced preparations; they do not amend a completed
certificate. The theorem permits Byzantine accepted proposers, changing
rosters and different certificate variants. Correct-signer provenance,
actual hydration and the common first-own-session predicate supply the
needed input coverage. The regional-service theorem supplies infinitely
many accepted sessions where its own hypotheses hold; neither pending
ingress alone nor a public-import occurrence asserts that service.
