## Independent rules

**E1 — Authentic finality is an independent gate.** v10 02 §§3.2, 3.10, 4.1, 4.5 and v2 §3.3 require exact descriptor/context, chain/config/version, target, view, message kind, historical ordered registry/PoP and four distinct legal signers. Three distinct signers or four entries with one duplicate do not suffice. An otherwise valid notarization, nullification or different-cid certificate is not finality. Missing verification basis is WAIT before the bool checker; malformed/known-invalid evidence is false/REJECT as appropriate. `Native` is provenance metadata, not a replacement for cryptographic checking. H1 must provide the actual verifier and artifact; no bool fixture can close this gate.

**E2 — Proof, regional delivery, readiness, publication, and G acceptance are different facts.** A verified target certificate can precede target/ancestor bodies. Complete delivery requires exact canonical hash-linked physical ancestry. Manager Ready additionally requires complete executed anchor state plus execution evidence, not a root or downloaded URL. Publisher Offer requires complete RegionalResult and exact AnchorEvidence. GFR after public selection needs a valid target proof and public physical bodies after its selected anchor; optional old AnchorEvidence does not move q_avail. H3 cannot treat a target hash as a full result or manufacture ReadyState from G head.

**E3 — Public completeness is historical.** v10 09 §§3.2, 3.4, 4.2, 4.7 define q_avail as the earliest q containing at least one valid target proof and every required physical body. Store each original `(q,message_index)`. Public absence permits replay with that result ineligible; a declared message missing locally stops the complete prefix. Already public origin/DEP/anchor execution/verifier material missing locally causes WAIT at the same event, not permanent rejection. A private cached body never fills a public hole. A valid descendant covers ancestors only through its complete physical chain; missing physical ancestors are not padding. Extra valid proof variants and later optional material cannot increase q_avail.

**E4 — Acceptance uses both public and replay coordinates.** Public cutoff requires `theta[q_avail] < session_end`; all new virtual heights, including padding below the new physical window, require `acceptance_key < ts*(h)+W_exp`. Equality fails. A prepublished unselected candidate keeps its original q_avail after selection but is checked at the later acceptance key and exact selected cid/anchor. Same height is not same anchor. G changes only by a fully validated contiguous append here. Invalid metadata, origin, parent or pre-state root must not be repaired by recalculation.

**E5 — Entire events are atomic.** v10 09 §§3.3–3.4, 4.1–4.3, 4.7 and frozen 07 §§6–8 require controls ≺ Expire ≺ SessionEnd ≺ Espresso at equal keys, then type index. Equal theta with distinct q is valid. Public complete Espresso and L1 control-list coverage authorize timers, never wall/local clock. At a single Espresso event, accepting h_max, closing predecessor, resolving successor, and accepting already cached successors produce one ReplayRecord. If any required later substep waits, no partial append, closure, assignment, publisher stop or manager allocation escapes that event.

**E6 — Regular keeps fixed windows.** After old [1,10], the successor is [11,20] whether the predecessor's last accepted physical height is 10, 8 or its initial anchor. Production anchor is that accepted terminal block; selection reference is the predecessor's initial anchor. SessionEnd must not wait for a never-produced h10, use the highest unaccepted candidate, append a synthetic h10, or later close the same session twice. M1 completes only after a genuine R2 result has traversed the whole path and entered G; SessionAssigned/Final/submission alone do not complete M1.

**E7 — Publisher retains durable tasks and controlled intent.** v10 04 §§3.4–3.5, PUB-1–3 and v2 §5.1 require verified complete result→durable task→durable stable attempt→root-controlled output custody/Ack→dispatch→service acknowledgement→delivered complete finalized receipt, with distinct facts and references. Component PREPARED is not root committed; service ACK is not RECORDED; RECORDED is not G accepted. Reoffers of the same ResultKey, including different valid proof bytes, do not replace the frozen verified payload or reopen a terminal task. No recovery is executed for healthy M1, but the live path must preserve durable state and leave no uncontrolled background output.

**E8 — Public stop is scoped and durable, including future offers.** v10 04 §3.4/PUB-3 and 03 §§3.1, 3.10, 4.1 require local TIME/Program Close to preserve publication attempts. Public SessionClosed, BranchClosed, or a different selected cid in the same session may stop only matching tasks. Full replay position+event kind+canonical body identify evidence. A bridge must retain public closure/selection facts so a late first Offer is stopped too; projecting affected keys only at event time misses that case. Repeated exact closures are idempotent; distinct events at one pc remain distinct. At deadline equality a complete reader watermark stops new attempts; header-only or another reader's watermark does not. Historical dispatched data can still arrive; a late verified complete receipt may change STOPPED→RECORDED without restarting attempts. A public stop cannot undo a root-committed prior dispatch; pending versus committed must be explicit.

**E9 — Canonical publication and framed transport have different limits.** Frozen 07 GFR-PUBLIC-1 wraps exact 03 canonical bodies/descriptors with unchanged proof bytes; codec `0x47465201`. Body/proof object separation changes transport of evidence, not signed payload identity. Frozen 06 limits a message to 65,536 bytes, encoded block to 1,048,576 bytes and count to 1,024. Exact 02 limits a transport payload to 65,536 bytes. Thus a maximum-sized message plus any positive fragment header cannot fit one payload. H1/H3 need a frozen transport fragmentation schema and ownership agreement; preserve bytes, original public q/index/count, full-object identity, bounded allocation and source/run binding. Missing/conflicting/duplicate/out-of-order fragments must not create a complete prefix or renumber public messages. This is transport reassembly, not a new public-object codec. Even an empty maximum-source-id PELG block has framing overhead.

**E10 — Readers are independently delivered.** v2 §11.2 and v10 09 require separate ReaderState, L1 reader, ReplayState, caches, queues and pc for A/B and every consuming local view. They may share immutable input fixtures, not mutable G or another reader's future deliveries. At common committed pc compare full public projections, not only head hash. Wall pause pairs compare canonical virtual traces; wall metadata is outside the equality domain.

**E11 — Official readback is separate evidence.** Exact 16 contract pins release `20260918`, official commit `fd221e8e4b51f5ecec0ae44bc99f6b7f82cd1171`, protocol `0.3`, HTTP `/v1`, namespace `160016`. Submit the exact M1 GFR-PUBLIC-1 payload bytes, record their SHA-256 and native-result/proof/production provenance, then verify complete official block/leaf and every flat tx position, namespace, q/hash/index and byte identity. Keep source_transaction_index separate from the filtered message_index. Trust remains TrustedLocalOfficialApi; QC/VID is captured but not verified. proposed/finalized/live timing stays Unknown and PE conversion UnsupportedTimingEvidence. An HTTP ACK is not complete readback. No official wall evidence enters the deterministic main trace or becomes PUB-4/E-TS evidence.

## Escalated assumptions and limits

- M0 integration SHA and H1 actual finality/controlled-output exports are not yet delivered. H2 lifecycle/ReadyState handshake is not frozen. No compatibility or compilation claim follows from API resemblance.
- Exact fragmentation framing/header sizes, object aggregate identity, capacity/backpressure behavior and owner are unresolved until frozen. The 64 KiB/1 MiB source bounds are facts, not a chosen fragment format.
- Durable Publisher intent is component capability; actual root commit/Ack custody and receipt propagation must be shown on the exact integrated revision. Pure ServiceGrant/GrantConsumer alone cannot prove that boundary.
- Lifetime of public stop facts must include late offers and repeated closures; a one-time `affected_keys` list is insufficient by itself. H2 and H3 must agree which durable state supplies and acknowledges these facts.
- Finite healthy trace progress needs an actual interval of four ready, entered, continuously scheduled replicas in the same cid, adequate state/body inputs, and declared network/work budgets. Five online processes or first Enter time alone does not establish this premise.
- No safety/liveness theorem, failure recovery, automatic Force, D/RESUME, native Espresso consensus proof, PUB-4 bound, production timing or complete I1 result is claimed. No seed/height/q bounds are fabricated for unrun integration.
