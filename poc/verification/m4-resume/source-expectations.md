## Real batch and independent D

1. Require an ordinary transaction that changes execution in a real accepted G block. Recover G and its acceptance position independently from actual public Espresso rows, native certificate and body/ancestor bytes. Follow its retained ordinary transactions into the exact versioned 11 frame and DA bytes. Verify the batch source heights, original G parent/position, prev/next cursor, range, commitment and actual DA receipt and Certifier attestation. Compare the Inbox call and complete successful finalized `CursorAdvanced` receipt at its exact global position. Submitted, pending, reverted or merely local cursor transitions cannot produce a D input.
2. Recompute D with only the pinned genesis, finalized canonical L1, accepted DA bytes, configuration/deposit schedule and previous derived blocks. The Certifier may check G. D uses the actual D parent and executes the selected kept transactions and L1 attributes/deposits on that parent. Establish a timely batch height with at least one retained ordinary transaction in D and canonical native bytes matching the applicable G height. Matching tips or a claimed input commitment alone do not demonstrate this equality.
3. For a different height with ordinary transactions in G, establish absence of an admissible batch through the complete native window. Under the pinned OP Batch Queue lateness predicate, `including_L1_height <= origin_height + sequence_window_size` is timely, subject to the other checks. Check equality and +1. An eventual late inclusion stays late regardless of DA download timing. Preserve the exact covering finalized inputs, queue state, and required next canonical origin. An Inbox/DA gap at a finalized accepted batch is WAIT at that position, never evidence of an empty native block. A wrong commitment or conflicting authenticated input must fail verification before any derived-state progress. Authentic data arriving later retries the same prefix.
4. Verify deposit-only fallback including deposits and execution root. Compare actual PoC native hashes at a shared height before RESUME. Find the longest equal native prefix and first divergence, checking actual parent linkage, metadata/origin, DEP, kept ordinary transactions and post-state root. D lag and D excess with equal shared prefix do not establish HASH_DIVERGENCE. The user requests a preset authorized control after the evidenced divergence. Automated observation and target choice belong to M5.

## L1 control and exact history

5. Verify actual governance threshold, signatures and full domain/request fields against calldata and deployed authority. The real coherent successful receipt contains exactly the corresponding `ResumeRecorded` and `CursorSet` at two actual positions in one transaction. Validate contract identity, signed control seq, policy, target height and origin, inclusion block and event pairing. The target height is strictly above the prior Inbox cursor. Override clears frame state and carries `RESUME(c)` source. The L1 cursor change occurs through this transaction at inclusion. It does not immediately change G.
6. Add a real rejected transaction which exercises CursorSet/Override failure (e.g. target no longer strictly above the inbox cursor). Verify cursor, control sequence, accepted request identity and execution logs remain at their old values. Use the contract’s actual storage/getters or a later successful attempt paired with the real revert evidence to establish rollback. Contrast a low but still contract-accepted target that is too old for useful GFR production. The latter must still align G.
7. Reconstruct the observation of D as each exact associated CursorSet is processed in finalized L1 position order. Before that boundary, execute preceding accepted data or WAIT at the first missing position. On CursorSet, reset pending channel state and fill absent heights through the target immediately with deposit-only blocks using canonical next origins from the actual parent. Keep D unchanged when D already reaches beyond the target. For same-block controls at distinct tx/log positions, compare their position, control seq, target, cut digest and per-control D history, then retry the earlier exact cut after D advances. End-of-block D and latest queried D cannot replace that cut. When a cut's derived prefix extends past its target, the anchor and state are selected at the target and G adopts only through that target.
8. Recompute the position-bound cut anchor separately from D native bytes, actual origin, and target post-state. Use the v10 derived-anchor identity from height/native hash, not the old G physical hash, a copied cursor-origin field, or the cursor's claimed target timestamp. Ensure the prefix is contiguous and that cut source binds the exact `ResumeRecorded` position and paired `CursorSet` boundary. Distinguish the two events' actual indexes and their processing order. A non-RESUME position or mismatched digest/target/material rejects, missing history waits.

## GFR and Manager

9. Compute activation public key `x = tsL1(including_block) + M_a` from raw L1, and independently locate Espresso's first key at least x. Full verified finalized L1 controls cover x−M_a, full verified Espresso covers x, and every transition validation input including the exact cut and any Plan history is available. At missing cutoff/DA/cut/Plan input, processing stops immediately before this event without position advance, G mutation, closure, assignment, early grant or later event effects. Restored evidence processes the original position. Compare independent A/B G, Tail, events and cut at equal replay positions under reordered delivery.
10. On executed valid RESUME, close the old branch even if its Tail is HALT or already diverged. Preserve the maximal common native prefix and its provenance. Replace the remaining G suffix with cut-derived blocks through target and mark the adopted suffix as DER. Provenance and matching native bytes/prefix are jointly checked. The new branch is old beta+1, session r=0, source region absent, exact window [target+1,target+10] and policy from this control. L1 Plan floor and Ref origin equal the actual cut anchor origin, so the historical Plan query is at that origin, not the including L1 height. Select the first eligible region in the new policy order. D, including any existing D-ahead suffix, retains its independent append-only history.
11. Replay order is (public key, category, position), with L1 controls (Force+RESUME) first by (block,tx,log), then Expire, SessionEnd and Espresso. Recompute timers after each L1 control. If the post-alignment next-height Expire or new-window SessionEnd is strictly before act, align G and report Resumed, immediately HALT with new SessionClosed(EXPIRED), skip Plan/creation and never insert a past timer. Equality is processed after all same-key L1 control(s) as a fresh timer if still applicable, possibly expiring an assigned duty. Distinguish obsolete old-branch expiry from an applicable new-duty timer and verify neither old publication nor pending work can sneak into the new context.
12. Freeze the authorized event batch order `BranchClosed(RESUME) → Resumed → SessionAssigned` for positive startup. Manager records terminal guards for every old-branch instance, including Preparing/Paused, before Close is issued. Resumed binds the same public position and exact cut anchor/material to recovery. Assignment and state material availability precede new Prepare/Create/Enter. Check no externally visible partial batch during a WAIT, and verify idempotent duplicate replay, old-cid late Prepare/Enter and release of no old-cid signatures or prepares after terminal. In low-target mode no new valid Prepare is issued and the terminal event retains the correct new session identity.
13. Confirm all 15 worker identities by bounded process evidence. Confirm independently staggered Ready/Enter and enough valid new committee participants before an accepted native Final for h_res+1. Check original clock RPC/grant/Ack boundaries and separation of public keys from receipt/virtual and wall times. Check the first successor's parent binds the derived anchor, valid origin is scheduled starting from actual anchor.os (can advance by at most one at first height), pre-state is target's actual post-state, and ordinary transactions execute through the resumed state. Prove exact descriptor/CID/domain and BLS Final, raw Espresso content/first complete position, actual GFR acceptance before the applicable expiry/cutoff and reexecuted output. Ready, assignment, or a private regional Final alone does not meet completion.
14. For the positive preset target, check the actual L1 inclusion and existing governance timing budget, including `tsL1(L)+T_prep <= ts*(h_res) <= tsL1(L)+T_drift−epsilon`, `T_prep >= M_a`, and `M_a+T_ready+delta_G <= T_prep+b_L2`, or the fully specified test-profile equivalents. Record the actor observation and measured virtual readiness margin, distinct from wall time. Confirm the target is beyond observed G/D/Inbox heads at submission for this positive and preserve exception cases as their own negative scenarios.
