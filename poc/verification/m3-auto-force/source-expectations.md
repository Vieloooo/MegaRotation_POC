## Decision and durable submission

For each governance signer, independently recover the ordering and control input prefix it actually verified, its complete GFR replay position and selected tail, its good clock status, and its latest complete Espresso observation. Reconstruct `x_begin = max(window_start, assignment_public_key)`, `x_progress = latest key at which the current selected cid appended new G content` or `x_begin` when the current cid made no contribution, and `x_obs = θ` of the latest completely processed Espresso event. The expected detector applies all of these guards at a poll and rechecks current responsibility and pending request state immediately before broadcast.

1. The selected responsibility is an open `NEXT` whose window has begun. PENDING is the no-eligible-plan case. HALT requires RESUME. An old assignment or old cid's accepted height cannot serve as evidence for a new responsibility.
2. Each input prefix through the observation is complete and verified. Espresso must cover the event's public key. The finalized L1 control prefix must cover `x_obs−M_a` and include all controls through the covering block. A missing required predecessor, locally missing body or verification material, or a replay pc behind a verified finalized head relevant to the poll is a wait. A block with a publicly incomplete result may still be processed. That result contributes zero G progress.
3. Ordering service liveness requires progress of both verified finalized `q` and `θ`, observed within the configured bound from public data. Two distinct keys contribute a watermark advance. A fresh sequence of publication heights at one frozen `θ` contributes zero watermark advance. A local elapsed timeout cannot substitute for public observations.
4. `x_obs−x_progress` is at least the configured `T_stall`. At `T_stall−1µs` expect no vote. At equality expect eligibility when every other guard passes. Invalid certificates, wrong or closed cid, duplicate blocks, unselected results, and incomplete public results do not refresh `x_progress`.
5. No unresolved governance control is in flight and no verified incompatible G/D or urgent RESUME condition is present when such a condition is modeled. The bounded recovery estimate is strict: `a_max + T_init + b_L2 + T_produce + T_pack + T_pub + T_E + Δ_ts < ts*(h_G+1)+W_exp`. Equality fails the bound. Exact terms and enforcement use the frozen test profile. A signer receives verified public views and service observations for this decision. Independent replay should establish the same vote authorization from those observations without consulting the fault script, future scheduler events or another reader's private state.

Each governance vote authorizes one exact payload. Derive the EIP-712 digest and recover each signer from signed bytes for the actual receipt using the frozen contract's canonical encoding and domain. Count distinct configured members who signed the identical request fields bound to the chain, Authority, operation, sequence, target branch and policy. Reject a single signature in default 2-of-3, repeated signer, outsider, changed signed fields, wrong chain or Authority, and a cross-operation replay. Confirm configured threshold from real deployment state. At least one real Anvil acceptance and real rejected threshold submission must show actual transaction/receipt semantics. Invalid execution leaves request, sequence, policy and control log available for the correct request. Request dedup follows the endpoint's frozen exact-return semantics and event count: reusing the same request identity and identical contents yields the same control with one canonical Force record total, conflicting contents fail even with threshold signatures, non-increasing sequence cannot add a control. Repeated polls and restart after an uncertain broadcast must preserve and recover the same pending request identity and signed transaction or a proven chain disposition. One outstanding request is retained until confirmed failure or GFR processes the exact record including a public no-op. Recovery monitoring after apply is a separate phase.

## Historical Plan and activation

The successful receipt establishes inclusion coordinates `(ℓ_U, tx, log)` and header timestamp, so `act=ts_L1(ℓ_U)+M_a`. That key is distinct from virtual apply and receipt read times. While only included or finalized, the control remains pending relative to GFR. GFR applies only when its own verified Espresso watermark covers `act`, a complete finalized L1 history covers `act−M_a`, and the necessary historical Plan materials are present. At equal key, order L1 control by position, Expire, SessionEnd, then Espresso by height. A valid control changes the branch atomically. With target branch closed or stale or current HALT, expect a recorded invalid/no-op control and no policy change, branch change, or G mutation. A same-branch change of cid between signing and activation does not invalidate the recorded control.

The new anchor and its executed state are the actual current G head immediately before applying the control. Old G persists, old branch closes all cids, and the new branch has `β=old β+1`. For prior NEXT, old physical contribution to G implies `r_new=r_old+1`, otherwise `r_new=r_old`, and source region is the prior responsibility. For prior PENDING, the saved boundary supplies the sequence and source region. Reconstruct the window exactly as `[max(A.h+1,slot(act+T_init)+1), h_min+9]`.

Install the signed region mask and region cycle on a valid public activation. Verify the new branch floor `λ=max(old floor,ℓ_U)` per v10 09 §4.4, and distinguish any separately signed envelope floor field according to the contract. Recompute `ℓ_s=max(A.origin,λ)` for the first Force Plan, correlate it to a verified historical Authority header/snapshot and policy reference, and require missing history to leave the event waiting. Genesis with no source starts at the first cycle entry. With a source, start strictly at its successor and traverse the whole cycle once. The first enabled and eligible region is selected, the stable full committee remains associated to that selected region, and current online membership is absent from selection input. All-regions-ineligible with complete history yields PENDING. A later policy re-enabling a region affects plans under that later activation and leaves historical descriptor validation anchored to the original policy, history and registry. Two independent readers at the same replay position must agree with each other and the source-derived plan. A later reader's state uses the same frozen `cid`, authorization evidence and committee for old signatures and results.
