SCOPE
Presentation revision of v4.4, plus fixes for ambiguous rules and unhandled
cases. The problems found and the plan are in revision-plan.md. No protocol
step was added, removed, or reordered. Section and protocol numbers are those
of v4.4. No independent reviewer or mechanized verification was used. The
security and complexity analyses remain deferred.

The default latexdiff style (ulem) fails on \Cref inside added text, so the
LuaLaTeX underline style is used. restore_positions.py puts Protocols 2 and 5
back at their v4.4 source positions. Without it, latexdiff shows the box move
as a deletion and re-insertion of Section 7.2.
