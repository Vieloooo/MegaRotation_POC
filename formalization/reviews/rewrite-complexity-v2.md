# Complexity and receiver re-audit — rewrite v2

Audited frozen source: `formalization/build/rewrite/review-v2.tex` (1,344 lines), against v1 and the complete repaired transport, regional/global algorithms and Section 7. No manuscript edits. Most v1 findings are resolved; two narrowly scoped qualifications remain.

## Remaining findings

1. **[P2] Qualify the optimistic `M` equality by the primary-push condition too.** Lines **569–573** now correctly share the content-digest body cache across wrappers and permit only one logical outstanding repair attempt. The general bound `B_body <= M_0 + (a+1) M_r` at **1213–1223** is sound under the stated attempt and response caps, and its reduction to `(a+2)M` is explicitly conditional on `M_0,M_r <= M`. However, the preceding unconditional wording at **1210–1212** says that cross-wrapper caching plus no repair race makes optimistic actual transmission exactly `M`. D1 (**553–556**) limits primary push once *per mini-block per producer*, not once per distinct body digest. Several mini-blocks can contain identical transaction-list bytes, including empty lists; they can therefore trigger repeated primary body pushes even though that body is already cached. Keep the general `M_0` accounting and qualify the optimistic equality with the same no-repeated-body-push condition, or consistently count body units per mini-block rather than by reusable content digest. This is a residual accounting qualification, not the old missing cross-wrapper-cache problem.

2. **[P2, workload qualification] `Q <= J_0` needs report-ready data and adoption epochs.** The variable-size packing issue is correctly fixed at **1055–1066**: nominal byte/count maxima are no longer presented as a service rate. The new `Q <= J_0` derivation nevertheless needs the next pending outcome to be available to an honest participating reporter at each counted snapshot. “Retrievable” data (**1055, 1060**) need not yet be present there: D3 can require sequential bounded pulls (**563–573**), and **1044–1053** distinguish one-range `F_G` from the sum for multiple chunks. `L_sync` at **1069** charges one `F_G`, so do not implicitly use it as the complete transfer of an arbitrary `J_0`-outcome backlog. Also count epochs eligible to adopt data; pending ROTATE/CONFIRM deliberately adopt nothing (**705–707, 717–724**). A minimal qualification is that `J_0` bounds an already reporter-ready contiguous backlog during uninterrupted adoption epochs; otherwise retain `Q` as an independent workload bound that includes these waits, or explicitly add the sum of necessary transfer times. The formulas conditional on a valid supplied `Q` are not disproved by this issue.

## V1 findings closed

- **Announcement propagation:** `T_A` is now explicitly an emission period (**232**); `A_R`, `A_G` and `A_P` include the relevant path delay (**1048–1053**). Their use in synchronization, delivery and recovery (**1069, 1097, 1125, 1146**) is consistent. `Delta_P` is the consumer announcement bound and `delta_P` the good-case complete-object push delay; their distinct uses are appropriate.
- **Continuous versus intermittent failure:** The `K_R` expression is explicitly limited to continuously unproductive tenures (**1118–1129**). The `N_R` replacement correctly covers intermittent Byzantine tenure (**1130–1135**); every tenure reaches the next fixed planned boundary within at most `N_R` instances. `L_start` and the minimum-tenure condition now use this conservative class (**1067–1078**).
- **Receiver authority replay:** The receiver persists its finalized anchor and highest generation (**428–430**); D6 rejects ancestor/replayed proofs, queues future activation and advances generation monotonically (**585–592**). D4 checks both the current generation and activation time (**574–579**). These address the v1 replay/early-activation guard gap.
- **Extra control traffic:** HEAD has a specified slot and send rule (**433–434, 454–463**), is included in the regional subtotal, and announcement, pull and evidence costs are parameterized separately over an interval (**1196–1204**). The control table is no longer implicitly the entire interval cost.
- **Payload races:** The extra primary-copy term and explicit `(a+2)M` condition (**1213–1224**) correctly avoid assuming cancellation can retract in-flight bytes. Late failed attempts are covered by the attempt-count factor.

## Other checks retained

- **READY to regional start:** The new healthy-successor condition `w(H_G intersect V_j)>2W_j/3` (**202–205**) closes the ambiguity between local health and globally synchronized readiness. True READY is mandatory when its condition holds (**665–672**); the selected baseline cannot advance during ROTATE/CONFIRM. Finality by `(N+2)Delta_G` with `T_G >= (N+3)Delta_G` still leaves a delay margin before the fixed activation boundary (**684–690, 756–775**).
- **Recovery endpoint:** The geographic bound explicitly ends at successor Preview (**1149–1153**), and adds the finalized-delivery bound only for Deliver. Tail, readiness and conditional transfer terms remain visible.
- **Control complexity:** The `O(n^3)` direct and `O(E n^2)` flooded transmission counts and `L+n kappa` envelope sizes remain consistent with lifetime cap-two forwarding (**1161–1195**). The proof-history parameter remains explicit.
- **Full-data latency and retention:** `2 delta_R` remains a complete-object good case (**1081–1092**), not a metadata-only delay. Cold synchronization still sums required chunks and includes retained evidence; storage units are now unambiguously bits (**1237–1243**).
