PROTOCOL BOUNDARY
- Fixed n=2f+1 committee and at most f permanently Byzantine members.
- Complete-message synchrony, including required history and validation, <=Delta.
- L1 schedule L(g,t) is finalized, immutable and available before entry.
- Any complete admissible current-generation quit proof freezes all generation
  timers; successor entry is always (g+1,0).
- Recovery uses SH STATUS, lock, leader-selected NEWVIEW and no-timer ANCHOR
  re-vote. It runs no agreement protocol over CBs and no recovery L1 transaction.
- A terminal certificate has f+1 post-timer DONE signatures, including at least
  one actual correct timer commitment. Imported commits cannot create DONE.
- Old proofs do not re-open completed recovery; future proofs wait for entry.

FINDINGS RESOLVED DURING REVIEW
1. Each p=1,...,K now has a separate explicit progress-check deadline.
   A partially completed K-block term cannot escape later deficiency checks.
2. Progress is counted by admitted positions supported by DATA votes or DATA
   QCs, rather than falsely requiring every node to vote every earlier block.
3. Certificate deduplication is semantic and includes type/view/rank.
   Different ranked certificates for the same block are not suppressed.
4. STATUS explicitly carries CB(g,*) as E_(g+1), removing witness-index ambiguity.
5. Recovery control counts exclude old DATA/DONE backlog charged to its
   originating blocks and terms.
6. Height dissemination and good-case timing distinguish terminal votes from
   already-known DONE. Timing uses first local processing, not redelivery.
7. References distinguish assumptions, lemmas and theorems correctly.
