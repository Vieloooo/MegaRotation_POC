# Final scope review

Reviewed the complete `attack-review.md` against the frozen `source/main.tex`, concentrating on live-stream stalls, L6/L7, and the handoff/consumer implications. No manuscript changes were made.

## Corrections before delivery

1. **L6's last causal step needs its selective-delay condition.** At review line 294, an arbitrary delayed honest forwarding packet is described as supplying L1's trigger. One delayed copy can be repaired by a different honest receiver's timely later forwarding. The supported statement is that overload can instantiate L1 **if it produces the selective complete-object deadline misses in L1**. The valid-traffic amplification is demonstrated; amplification alone does not prove a stalled or split epoch. The surrounding table and scope labels already make this distinction correctly.
2. **Global stall claims should name honest/accepted output.** Phrases such as “all new batches stop” or “disables every scheduled producer” should refer to honest new batch origination and accepted current-authority output. A Byzantine identity can still sign or transmit stale-anchor objects. The source's fail-closed rule governs honest behavior and acceptance, not the adversary's ability to emit packets. This does not weaken the demonstrated service deadlock.
3. **Inline math needs presentation repair.** Much of the review uses literal `(V_i)`, `(H_G)`, and raw TeX such as `(W_i=\sum_{S\in V_i}w_S)` without math delimiters. Review line 244 also contains `(N\)-round`. This is a readability defect in an otherwise self-contained report.

## Verified conclusions

- **L1:** the deadline trace is sound under the stated single temporary timing-premise violation and continued Byzantine abstention. In particular, forwarding once prevents self-delivery from creating a rescue chain, different report-vector commitments force different FFG targets, and persistent same-epoch voting prevents convergence after healing. It is correctly labeled outside the uninterrupted global envelope.
- **Cross-layer halt:** frozen source lines 531–539 prohibit a node missing the due checkpoint from originating batches, HEAD, or REPORT using a stale anchor. Lines 556–579 require the immediately preceding justified parent for the next global epoch. Lines 141–145 and 634–643 make geographic activation dependent on global finality. Local clock advancement and geographic switching therefore do not bypass L1.
- **Consumer scope:** lines 511–527 support pausing current-authority output when required proof or bytes are missing. Already finalized historical catch-up can continue when its required objects are available. The review distinguishes these cases.
- **L2–L5:** the quorum arithmetic, two-epoch depth-one production gap, weighted-identity finite silence, and undersized-tenure infinite rotation trace are correct. L5 explicitly violates the minimum-tenure premise; L3 and L4 are permitted pauses, not theorem refutations.
- **L6:** two private values per honest endpoint can yield `2H` distinct reports for one Byzantine origin, all forwarded by honest endpoints. Complete-object verification precedes local cap-two discard; with distinct bodies, aggregate endpoint payload work can reach `Theta(H^2 b)` per origin. Repeating over `f` origins gives the stated scaling when the configuration and admission budget permit it. The local producer-slot candidate cap does not constrain global report validation. This matches source lines 315–318, 383–388, 1075–1087, 1094–1116, and 1192–1202.
- **L7:** mandatory retention includes accepted report bodies whose origins become bottom or whose forks lose. Repeated fresh orphan data therefore consumes any fixed additional storage allocation. The source explicitly declines a bounded-storage guarantee. The review correctly treats disk exhaustion as a conditional finite-resource deployment failure, not an in-model liveness counterexample.
- **Rejected attacks:** READY binds the parent tip; ROTATE/CONFIRM adopt no candidate extension; cyclic readiness cannot skip a continuously ready region forever; catch-up preserves delivered entries and rejects ancestor rollback. Their source citations accurately support the scope labels.

No additional must-fix mathematical or source-scope defect was found. The final document prioritizes the user's requested deadlocks and live-stream stalls and does not misrepresent ordinary preview reorgs as finalized-prefix attacks.
