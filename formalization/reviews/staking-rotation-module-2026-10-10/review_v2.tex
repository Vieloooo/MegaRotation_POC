\section*{Detailed verification log}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=7pt]
\item \textbf{Churn and penalty accounting.}
Lines 141--144 restrict amount budgets to voluntary queued changes.
Lines 163--174 omit incumbents below the minimum remaining balance,
record mandatory departures, charge voluntary exits by old frozen weight,
and construct positive successor weights. The penalty counterexample to
v1 no longer contradicts the claimed bound. Post-freeze debit effects are
expressly separated from historical verification and collateral adequacy.

\item \textbf{Request and membership lifecycle.}
Lines 154--176 prohibit refund after reservation, remove canceled records,
and consume selected requests at freeze. Lines 317--320 promote selected
joins, retire recorded departures, clear the plan and reset the actual
epoch-start timestamp. A new member can subsequently request exit;
the same plan cannot be applied twice.

\item \textbf{Idle and missing-plan branches.}
Lines 321--326 set the result to idle and skip selection when a due plan
is unready. An idle predecessor or a newly installed epoch selects the
first eligible region; only a non-idle within-epoch transition scans after
the predecessor region. Empty eligible sets produce idle. Lines 330--331
reject an idle advance that changes neither configuration nor authority.

\item \textbf{Unique authority and simultaneous triggers.}
Lines 312--316 check the expected counter/session, process due control
events, authenticate the trigger, and verify terminal closure. An idle
step retains exactly the stored anchor. Lines 329--334 atomically consume
the predecessor and record the successor. The exact-session off latch
survives a later live update until retirement. These rules serialize
epoch expiry, quota expiry, and status changes in the finalized L1 log.

\item \textbf{Prefix preservation and delayed receipts.}
The external closure contract preserves all old finalized or finalizable
prefixes and excludes later old-session continuation. Opening requires
the finalized authority and checkpoint data. Lines 340--347 check the
original authorization, native historical finality, opening anchor and,
for a closed session, inclusion in the recorded closing prefix.
Proposition 1 therefore follows by induction over authority transitions.

\item \textbf{Actual retirement and timely claims.}
Lines 177--187 start the liability clock at actual configuration retirement.
Claims accepted on L1 at or before the evidence deadline remain effective;
release occurs strictly after it and excludes encumbered funds.
Attribution to the position/signing domain and exactly-once payment
preserve historical responsibility. Proposition 2 follows under the
external adjudication contract and makes no claim to replenish prior losses.

\item \textbf{Progress and randomness scope.}
Lines 407--423 distinguish installing a configuration from opening and
serving an eligible region. FIFO and withdrawal progress additionally
require continued installations, maturity, positive budgets and dependency
progress. Frozen plans precede the one accepted seed; no fairness theorem
independent of committee filtering or operator behavior is asserted.
\end{enumerate}
