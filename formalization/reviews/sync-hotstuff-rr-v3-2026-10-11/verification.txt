AUTHORIZED VARIANT
The user requested an optimized v3 with immediate terminal-QC rotation and
clarified that a term allows at least one batch, potentially more (K >= 1).
The user added prospective PoS exclusion of proven equivocating sequencers.
Normally completed terms certify K fresh batches; faulty terms may produce
none and require recovery. Positive quota does not force Byzantine output.

PROTOCOL AND MODEL BOUNDARY
- Fixed n=2f+1 voting committee, at most f permanent Byzantine identities.
- Complete correct-to-correct processing, including dependencies, <= Delta.
- Finalized immutable L1 authority specifies L(g,t); recovery waits for both
  the Delta drain interval and the next authority's availability.
- Normal handoff uses the terminal DATA QC immediately. Existing commit timers
  survive; no DONE certificate or predecessor commitment gates successor votes.
- One complete admissible quit proof freezes all current-generation timers.
  Every recovery targets (g+1,0), using SH entry locks, STATUS, NEWVIEW, and
  timerless ANCHOR re-certification.
- A valid current-generation DATA QC starts a 2Delta catch-up timer when the
  replica has no timer or commitment for that key. This also covers embedded
  DATA QCs in admitted ancestry. ANCHOR and old-generation QCs do not start it.
- PoS removal changes future proposer eligibility, not voting membership.
  Every revealed equivocator has a finite generation cutoff beyond which it
  is never scheduled. Fault identities cannot reenter or be replenished.
- L1 authority, evidence inclusion, finality and epoch activation must progress
  independently of L2 commitment. Schedule and input fairness are explicit.
- A positive physical message-delay lower bound is not used in the main
  safety or eventual-progress proof. Explicit pacing is an optional profile.

FINDINGS RESOLVED DURING REVIEW
1. Correct replicas learning only DATA QCs now obtain local catch-up timers;
   the proof does not assume every replica DATA-votes every earlier block.
2. Initial entry skew and common authority/genesis are explicit safety premises.
3. Correct proposers immediately self-process and vote their own fresh block.
4. Recovery entry is max(local freeze + Delta, authority readiness), preserving
   Delta entry skew without assuming the next L1 record was prepublished.
5. Finite exclusion bounds generation indices named by revealed proofs;
   it does not falsely bound interruptions by f or assume instant slashing.
6. The final-generation proof treats terminal-QC knowledge, terminal voting,
   and deficient progress checks separately.
7. The optional pacing profile prohibits earlier exposure of signed proposals
   through QCs/history and specifies compatible progress-check deadlines.
8. Partial BLAME traffic is charged per term; L1 cost is separate from local
   post-freeze recovery communication.
9. Exclusion failure and authority-availability failure have distinct stated
   consequences; the perpetual-equivocation example is not claimed for both.

MAIN CHECKED CONCLUSIONS
- Safety/validity depend on authenticated synchrony and unique authority,
  not fairness, exclusion, or a message-delay lower bound.
- A DATA timer and complete relay at s, with no freeze through s+3Delta,
  gives local commitment by s+2Delta and all-correct commitment by s+3Delta.
- Absence of newly elected Byzantine leaders alone does not exclude a delayed
  proof of an older current-generation equivocation.
- After equivocation generations cease, a correct initializer's first block
  commits at all correct replicas even if QC rotation reaches a withholding
  Byzantine successor; its earliest honest timeout complaint is too late.
- Eventual exclusion, schedule fairness and the fixed fault set give eventual
  unbounded committed height. Fair input service then gives request liveness.
- Optional explicit pacing gives pre-handoff local/all-correct commitment
  under (K-1)rho > 2Delta / (K-1)rho > 3Delta, respectively.

LIMITS
These are mathematical reviews of an explicit abstract specification, not
mechanized verification or deployment certification. The voting committee
remains fixed; stake-weighted or dynamic membership needs a separate argument.
Eventual L1 exclusion/readiness does not supply a uniform latency or numerical
bound on aborted generations. Communication counts admitted protocol traffic,
not arbitrary Byzantine flooding; complete-history byte costs remain explicit.
